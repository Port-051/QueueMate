Use case: logo-brand. Design one standalone SYMBOL for QueueMate, a gaming teammate matching service. NO WORDMARK, NO TEXT, NO LETTERING beside it. Core idea must stay: TWO OVERLAPPING SPEECH BUBBLES whose combined silhouette and negative space form a clear capital Q. Rebuild the geometry elegantly from scratch. Two compact rounded-square chat bubbles, one slightly above-left and the other below-right, overlap and interlock naturally around one generous shared rounded-square negative space. The lower-right bubble's short integrated speech tail doubles as the Q tail. The upper-left bubble's small speech tail is tucked into the overlap, so the outside silhouette remains clean. Make the relationship of the two bubbles unmistakable without literal people, eyes, dots or chat lines. Design principles: simplicity, Gestalt closure, balanced visual weight, consistent corner radii and band thickness, generous negative space, smooth tangent-continuous curves, memorable silhouette at 24px, usable in one color. Avoid random seams, sharp pinches, tiny notches, ribbon folds or an overly mechanical broken ring. Two flat complementary violet tones: #8755F6 for upper-left and #AA82FF for lower-right, overlap indicated by a single clean intentional gap, no gradients. Friendly, confident, contemporary; crisp vector-like graphic. Square canvas, center symbol at about 68% of canvas width with balanced clear space. SOLID PURE BLACK #000000 background and all negative spaces. No checkerboard, texture, lighting, 3D, gloss, shadows, white outlines, watermark, explanatory labels, mockups, border or app-icon container. Exactly ONE large symbol and nothing else.
