## 생성 프롬프트

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: standalone project symbol logo for QueueMate, a teammate matching service.
Primary request: Design one beautifully resolved minimal geometric mark made from TWO OVERLAPPING SPEECH BUBBLES with the OVERLAPPING AREA CUT OUT. Their combined silhouette must immediately read as the capital letter Q. The empty center is the actual shared intersection of the two bubbles, not a third element drawn on top.
Geometry: two large, very rounded compact speech-bubble bodies, the first slightly upper-left and the second slightly lower-right, overlap substantially into one compact almost circular silhouette. Remove their common central area to create a generous clean rounded negative-space counter. The remaining two crescent-like bodies interlock around this empty center. The lower-right bubble has a short, decisive 45-degree down-right speech tail integrated into its outer contour, becoming the Q descender. A small inward-pointing tail for the upper-left bubble gives a subtle second speech cue without adding another outer protrusion. The two pieces have beautifully balanced optical weight and smooth continuous curves. Only purposeful minimal separations at the two intersections. The entire mark should feel rounder and more compact than two diagonally stacked square cards.
Style: refined contemporary identity design, crisp vector-like edges, flat two-color geometry, confident friendly silhouette, legible as Q at small sizes.
Palette: upper-left shape flat violet #8755F6, lower-right shape flat lighter violet #AA82FF. No gradient within either shape.
Composition: exactly ONE large centered symbol, square canvas, symbol occupies about 68 percent of canvas width with generous clear space.
Background: genuinely transparent background and genuinely transparent central cutout and gaps, alpha transparency. No simulated checkerboard.
Constraints: no wordmark, text, labels, letters printed separately, dots, eyes, mascot, app-icon container, border, shadow, glow, bevel, 3D, texture, watermark, construction lines, mockup, presentation board, or multiple logo variants. Avoid generic chain-link/infinity symbolism. Prioritize the two speech bubbles, their void intersection, and a clear Q silhouette.
